Bad Credit? No Problem!
opened for pre-order.

The iOS pre-order announcement that preceded the game’s August 8, 2024 launch.

July 17, 2024Robert FoorMobile launch

This article is preserved as part of the development archive. The game has been released and no longer requires a pre-order.

In July 2024, Bad Credit? No Problem! became available to pre-order on the iOS App Store.

The game placed players behind the desk at Title 4 Cash as a part-time title loan agent. Over a 14-day probation period, players would review documents, make approval decisions, and try to satisfy a demanding boss without ignoring the consequences for applicants.

About the game

Surviving probation unlocked Endless Shift, an escalating survival mode with more applicants each day. The game was designed as an ad-free experience focused on paperwork, characters, dark humor, and difficult decisions.

The original announcement confirmed an August 8 release date and invited players to reserve the game before launch.

Available now

The iOS edition can now be downloaded directly from the App Store.
