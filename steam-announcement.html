Bad Credit? No Problem! was confirmed for an October 2, 2025 PC release.

September 10, 2025RobBad Credit? No Problem!

This announcement was originally published before the Steam release. The game is now available.

After its mobile debut in August 2024, Bad Credit? No Problem! was headed to Steam with a release date of October 2, 2025.

The PC edition kept the same offbeat title-loan premise while adapting the experience for desktop controls and Steam Deck. Players would still spend a 14-day probation reviewing applications, following shifting rules, dealing with a demanding boss, and deciding whether desperate applicants qualified for a loan.

Planned Steam features

  • Desktop controls and interface improvements
  • Steam Deck support
  • The complete probation story
  • Endless Shift after earning a full-time position
  • Pixel art, office sounds, and dark comedy

The announcement marked the first move from mobile to PC for Foorbytes and set the stage for the October launch.
