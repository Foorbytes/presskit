Paperwork, pressure, and questionable business practices.

Overview

Bad Credit? No Problem! is a simulation game set inside Title 4 Cash, a title loan agency in 2016. As the newest part-time employee, the player must survive a 14-day probation by reviewing loan applications and making decisions against an ever-evolving rulebook.

Every application asks the player to balance accuracy, speed, company expectations, and the uncomfortable human consequences of the job. Success earns full-time employment and unlocks Endless Shift for extended play.

Key features

  • A complete 14-day story mode built around a probationary job
  • Paperwork inspection and rule-based decision making
  • An evolving rulebook that changes how applications are judged
  • Dark humor and social commentary on the title loan industry
  • Endless Shift mode for continued play after the story
  • Pixel art presentation and an immersive office soundscape
