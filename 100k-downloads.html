Bad Credit? No Problem! reached its first major download milestone less than a month after launch.

August 30, 2024RobBad Credit? No Problem!

Only a few weeks after its August 8 launch, Bad Credit? No Problem! passed 100,000 downloads.

The game was reaching players in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, and other territories while maintaining an average rating of 4.7 stars. Early App Store data showed nearly 12 million impressions, roughly 580,000 product-page views, and strong repeat engagement.

Players were connecting with the mix of paperwork, strategy, moral pressure, and dark humor. The response showed that a small game about title loans could find a much larger audience than expected.

What was next

The milestone announcement also previewed the upcoming Fright Night Files event, which would add a Halloween theme, new challenges, and rewards later that year.
