Frequently asked questions

What platforms are currently supported?
Bad Credit? No Problem! is currently promoted and supported on Steam and the iOS App Store. The Android version is no longer promoted or supported.
Does the Steam version support Steam Deck?
Yes. Steam Deck support is an official feature of the Steam edition.
Does the game support controllers?
No. Controller support is not currently available.
Does the game contain advertisements?
No. Bad Credit? No Problem! does not contain ads.
Does the iOS version include in-app purchases?
Yes. The iOS edition includes in-app purchases.
What is Endless Shift?
Endless Shift is the continuing mode unlocked after completing the main probation story. The workload keeps increasing as the player tries to survive as long as possible.
Is Fright Night Files still included?
Yes. The Fright Night Files Halloween content remains part of the current game.
Where should I report a bug?
Use the dedicated support and bug-report channel in the official Foorbytes Discord. Include your platform, what happened, what you expected to happen, and any screenshot or video that may help reproduce the issue.
Can I request a Steam review key?
Creators and media can use the website contact form or email foorbits@icloud.com. Include your channel, publication, or project and a link to your work.

Still need help?

Discord is the best place for game-specific support because reports can stay in the dedicated channel and include follow-up details. Email is also available for account, media, or private questions.
