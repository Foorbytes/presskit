The first post introducing the studio, its logo, and Bad Credit? No Problem!

July 17, 2024Robert FoorStudio news

The original Foorbytes blog opened with a simple goal: make games that are fun, engaging, and willing to explore unusual subjects.

A one-person studio

Foorbytes is the indie game development name used by Rob Foor. The studio was created as a home for personal projects built around distinctive ideas, straightforward gameplay, and stories that do not always fit the usual genres.

The logo

The pixel-inspired logo reflects the small pieces that make up a finished game. The aim was to keep the identity simple, recognizable, and connected to game development.

Introducing Bad Credit? No Problem!

The first major Foorbytes title placed players inside Title 4 Cash as a part-time loan agent trying to pass a 14-day probation. Applications, shifting rules, a demanding boss, and the needs of desperate clients created the game’s central tension.

Passing probation unlocked Endless Shift, where each day brought more applicants and more pressure. The game was designed around offbeat subject matter, dark humor, and decisions that mattered beyond a simple correct or incorrect answer.
