A historical announcement from the mobile launch period of Bad Credit? No Problem!

July 19, 2024RobMobile launch

This is a historical announcement. The Google Play version is no longer promoted on the current Foorbytes site.

In July 2024, Bad Credit? No Problem! opened for pre-registration on Google Play ahead of its mobile launch.

The game invited players to work as a part-time title loan agent at Title 4 Cash. Each day involved reviewing applications against an evolving rulebook while handling pressure from management and the needs of applicants.

What players could expect

  • A 14-day probation story mode
  • Endless Shift after earning a full-time position
  • Pixel art and immersive office sounds
  • Dark humor and commentary on the title loan industry
  • Decisions that could help or harm the people applying for loans

Pre-registration allowed players to be notified when the Android version became available. The announcement was part of the final lead-up to the August 2024 mobile release.

Current versions

The currently promoted editions are available on Steam and the iOS App Store.
