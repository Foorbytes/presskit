Fright Night Files
released.

A cursed playing card trapped Title 4 Cash inside an endless Halloween.

October 1, 2024RobBad Credit? No Problem!

The first major event for Bad Credit? No Problem! brought a Halloween curse, a new backlog of applicants, and a much stranger shift at Title 4 Cash.

A mysterious visitor leaves a cursed playing card on the desk. From that point forward, the office is stuck in an endless Halloween until every applicant is processed or the employee gets fired.

What the update added

  • Fright Night Files: a Halloween event with an eerie new atmosphere and an endless applicant challenge
  • PayHack: a tool that converts weekly income into monthly totals automatically
  • Interface improvements: clearer paperwork and streamlined bank transactions
  • Gameplay refinements: additional fixes and quality-of-life changes

At the time of the update, the game had passed 200,000 downloads and was carrying a 4.7-star average rating. The expansion gave returning players another reason to clock in during October.

Fright Night Files is included

The Halloween event remains part of the current game.
