Bad Credit? No Problem!
launches on Steam.

The paperwork simulation arrived on PC with rebuilt controls, expanded content, and Steam Deck support.

October 2, 2025RobBad Credit? No Problem!

This article was originally published on October 2, 2025. References to the launch discount are historical.

Bad Credit? No Problem! officially launched on Steam, bringing Title 4 Cash and its uncomfortable stack of loan applications to PC.

Players work inside a shady title loan office, checking applications, spotting errors, and making decisions that affect both their job and the people waiting across the desk. The game combines pixel art, office ambience, dark comedy, and a narrative built around the consequences of routine paperwork.

What came to Steam

  • Narrative simulation gameplay with choices and ethical pressure
  • A 14-day probation story followed by Endless Shift
  • Updated controls and interface for desktop play
  • Steam achievements, leaderboards, and Steam Cloud
  • Steam Deck support

The Steam version was the first Foorbytes PC release and introduced a new audience to the strange world of Title 4 Cash.

Available on Steam

The launch week has passed, but the full PC version remains available.
